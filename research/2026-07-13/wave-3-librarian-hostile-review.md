## Convergence verdict

## Fatal issues found and corrected

1. The verified-claim allowlist was empty. Only a reduced claim set meeting source/counter/date requirements will be promoted.
2. First-person rendering is not semantics-neutral; it changes speaker and can imply present endorsement. Public output is now dated third person.
3. Missing `valid_to` means no later observation, not a current belief. The architecture now uses `latest_observed` and `unknown_current` unless an explicit reaffirmation rule applies.
4. Completeness is only relative to the owner-declared signed scope and cutoff. PDF/fixity cannot prove undeclared, historical, deleted or platform-omitted items.
5. Restore semantics conflicted with deletion. Corrected rule restores only the active authorized snapshot after replaying tombstones/revocations; deleted content remains absent or inaccessible.
6. A one-sided 95% lower bound of 0.98 cannot be cleared by 100/100 cases. The harness now requires pre-registered, cluster-aware sample sizes; 149 independent successes is the minimum all-success case.
